## One network. Two purpose-built experiences.

| For consumers | For organizations |
| --- | --- |
| **A secure personal Consumer ecoPortal™** for responding to notices, reviewing offers, negotiating terms, managing payment plans, and keeping records in one place. | **A scalable Creditor ecoPortal™** that adds a digital resolution channel alongside existing billing, servicing, and collection operations. |
| Personal ecoAddress™ and account matching<br>Secure ecoMailbox™ and documented responses<br>Digital offers, counteroffers, disputes, and payment plans<br>Universal Bill Pay Wallet™ and Bill Pay Gift Links™ | Flexible account imports and system integrations<br>Structured consumer response and negotiation workflows<br>Communication, expiration, and compliance controls<br>Portfolio activity, payments, and documented reporting |
| [**Access the Consumer ecoPortal →**](https://consumer.younegotiate.com/) | [**Access the Creditor ecoPortal →**](https://creditor.younegotiate.com/) |

> ### Support can be part of the path
>
> Bill Pay Gift Links™ let consumers share an eligible balance, settlement, or active payment plan with trusted friends and family. Gift payments are sent through the participating creditor's payment flow and applied directly to the account.

## Security & trust

YouNegotiate uses a security-first architecture with encryption in transit and at rest, role-restricted access, and documented controls. The platform is hosted within Amazon Web Services and is architected to align with recognized frameworks, including ISO 27001 and PCI DSS, according to service scope and configuration.
